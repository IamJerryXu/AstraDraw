# 首页欢迎插画

![欢迎插画](welcome-header.png)

这张是首页单独使用的欢迎插画，不计入 48 个图标，也不在四张素材页的 ZIP 中。使用内置 imagegen 生成，延续 [基础图标](research-icons.png) 中的小鼠、雪花和弧形箭头，原图的来源记录保留。

## 绘图提示

> A welcoming banner for a friendly academic drawing workflow. Continue the original kawaii icon family: a small tan mouse with a blue winter scarf, icy blue, cream, coral and amber colors, and confident dark-blue outlines. One horizontal vignette on pure white, 3:1. The mouse draws three small colored blocks and a curved arrow on a sheet of paper with a blue pencil. A friendly snowflake and coral video tile sit on the left. A blue swooping arrow, modest golden star and small curved-grid tile sit on the right. Large coherent shapes, warm expression, restrained flat cel shading and white highlights. No words, title, panels, checkerboard, watermark or unrelated characters.

私人参考素材没有附带或嵌入仓库。这是生成的插画，不是逐对象可编辑的矢量组件。
