# Paper method diagram

The final three-column illustration developed for a research paper, shared with the author's permission. The public copy uses the neutral label **State reasoner** and preserves the original composition, colors, mathematical notation, real video frames and editable diagram elements.

The first slide combines an input-to-output overview with a magnified Propose/Verify mechanism and a state transition. The second slide isolates the transition for closer inspection and editing. Text, connectors, probability cells, gates and mask contours remain native PowerPoint objects. The real photographs remain separate replaceable pictures.

This is the final paper design, not the separate workflow reconstruction example. Public preparation only changes the method's display name and removes private manuscript metadata.

## Reading the figure

Blue and green distinguish the two shared-weight reasoning passes. Navy intensity encodes illustrative association probability, while coral highlights the contested observation column. The action strip follows Keep, Update, Revive and Suppress. The internal candidate gates are schematic, and the final state write uses the verified gate.

The photographs come from DAVIS. The colored masks use official ground-truth annotations to explain the output interface; they are **not model predictions**. Probabilities are illustrative, not experimental measurements.
