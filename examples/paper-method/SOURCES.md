## DAVIS photographs and annotations

- Dataset: [DAVIS 2017](https://davischallenge.org/davis2017/code.html), semi-supervised training split, `dogs-scale` sequence.
- Original video linked in the official dataset source list: [dogs-scale source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HhxyxHB8lFM).
- Frames shown: `00000`, `00020`, `00040`. The final frame also appears in the output illustration.
- The JPEG photographs preserve the original dataset pixels. The colored output overlays and editable mask contours derive from the official reference annotations for dog identities 1 and 2. Other annotated foreground objects are omitted for the explanatory diagram. These overlays are **not model predictions or experimental results**.
- Source archive: [DAVIS-2017-trainval-480p.zip](https://data.vision.ee.ethz.ch/csergi/share/davis/DAVIS-2017-trainval-480p.zip).
